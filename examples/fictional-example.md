# Fictional example: competitor analysis

Entirely fictional demonstration. All names, plans, prices, observations, and sources below are invented. No public websites were checked and no real market conclusion follows.

Decision: Explore positioning for fictional tutor appointment tool LanternDesk.

Synthetic source F1: invented ClearSlot product brief, demo date 2026-10-01, claims booking links for tutors; fictional price USD 12/month per tutor before tax, monthly billing, no verified usage cap.
Synthetic source F2: invented PaperPlan brief, same demo date, describes a free manual calendar and messaging workflow.
Synthetic source F3: invented LanternDesk internal brief, same demo date, proposes shared rescheduling but has no tested implementation.

Observation within the invented materials: F1 emphasizes initial booking; F2 requires manual coordination. Rescheduling is not mentioned in F1, which does not prove its absence.
Hypothesis: schedule-change coordination could be a useful positioning angle for LanternDesk.
Counterevidence: customer demand is unknown; ClearSlot could already support this capability.
Recommendation: verify competitor rescheduling documentation and interview potential buyers before writing any superiority claim. Do not publish “the only tutor scheduling tool” or a guaranteed time-saving promise.
