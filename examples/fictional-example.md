## Recommendation and claim boundary

Investigate whether **knowing both parties accepted the same replacement time** is a useful problem to address. This is a bounded question from F3, not a discovered market gap. F4 counters a broad “only tool with rescheduling” angle.

Possible discovery wording: “When a lesson moves, how do you confirm the final time with the parent?” This is a research question, not publish-ready product copy. Do not publish “the only tutor scheduling tool,” “unmatched confirmation,” or a guaranteed time-saving promise.

Next proposed check, **not performed**: compare the actual change-and-confirmation sequence in permitted documentation or a permitted walkthrough of real alternatives, recording source/date and unobserved areas. Drop the differentiation hypothesis if the behavior is already common. Even a documented gap still requires LanternDesk implementation proof and customer discovery before a public benefit claim.
